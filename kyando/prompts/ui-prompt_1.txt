Design a mobile-first web app called Kyandō. Coaches use it to run a recreational sports camp while the camp is happening. It is not for registration or booking beforehand.

Users:
About 20 coaches. They are the only users; campers have no accounts.
Roles include Head Organizer, Judo Coach and Polysport Coach.
Head Organizers create and edit the schedule. Everyone else can only view it.

Context of use:
Coaches use it mostly on their phones during a busy camp week (usually one week), often quickly and between sessions.
It's a web app, not a native app.

Core screens and features
Login-Screen: with E-Mail and Password.
Camp week calendar: the full week's schedule with day and time blocks, activity name, location and group. It must be easy to read on a small screen. Offer day and week views.
"My sessions today": a personal view of the sessions the logged-in coach is assigned to. This is the most important screen and probably the home screen.

Language:
The app supports several languages, with German as the default. Design all UI copy in German first.
Include a language switcher, and allow for longer German words in the layout.

Design system:
Use a Claude Design System based on shadcn/ui (https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui) components and conventions.

Out of scope
No payments
No camper accounts
No native app

What I'd like from you
The key screens and navigation structure
Layouts for "My sessions today", the calendar (day and week)
